ActionLayer

The operating layer your business is missing. Calls, documents, notes and meetings go in. ActionLayer indexes them and turns them into searchable insights, decisions and action items, and you can ask it questions in plain language, like you would ask a coworker.

Visit actionlayer.replit.app

Who it is for

Built for one kind of person.

Small and mid-size businesses whose know-how is buried in sales calls, proposals, job notes and reports.

  • Business knowledge
  • AI search

How it works

What it does, step by step.

  1. 1Upload files (PDF, Word, text, CSV) or paste text, and say what kind of material it is.
  2. 2AI pulls out summaries, action items, risks, opportunities and follow-ups.
  3. 3Results land in a searchable vault and an insights dashboard.
  4. 4An Ask page answers questions about your own material.
  5. 5It can pull in call transcripts from the call-tracking service Marchex.
