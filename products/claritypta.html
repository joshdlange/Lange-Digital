ClarityPTA

Plain answers for PTA volunteers. An AI helper that answers Texas PTA leaders' questions about governance, fundraising, meetings, money handling and compliance, in English or Spanish, grounded in official guidance.

Visit claritypta.com
claritypta.com

Who it is for

Built for one kind of person.

Texas PTA officers and volunteers who need a quick, correct answer about how their PTA should run.

  • PTAs
  • AI assistant
  • English and Spanish

How it works

What it does, step by step.

  1. 1Ask in plain language, or start from the questions PTA leaders ask most: handling money safely, meeting minutes, fundraising rules, filing a 990, officer vacancies, running a vote.
  2. 2Choose a quick answer or a detailed explanation.
  3. 3Switch between English and Spanish.
  4. 4Save the transcript to keep the answer.

What it shows

An assistant that answers from one organization's own guidance, so volunteers stop asking the same questions.
