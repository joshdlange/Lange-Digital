Trillage

Where school communities back what matters. Supporters subscribe to the teachers, PTAs, boosters and clubs they care about. The school side gets steady backing all year, and supporters get the Deal Book of local offers as a thank you. No bake sales or candy drives.

Visit trillage.io Open the app
trillage.io

Who it is for

Built for one kind of person.

Teachers, PTAs, boosters and clubs that raise money, the neighbors who back them, and the local businesses that want to reach those families.

  • Fundraising
  • Schools
  • Local businesses

How it works

What it does, step by step.

  1. 1Teachers set up a classroom fund backed by a virtual card.
  2. 2Supporters subscribe to a fund and unlock a book of local deals.
  3. 3Businesses list their own deals, choose which schools can see them, and get coupon analytics and A/B testing of their offers.
  4. 4Members save deals, get recommendations, make one-time donations and download tax receipts.
  5. 5An admin panel runs users, funds, payouts and analytics, and brand deals sync in daily from an affiliate network.

What it shows

Payments, subscriptions, payouts and a back office, working together in one system.
