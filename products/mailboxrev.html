Mailbox Rev

Someone should pay you. Mailbox Rev finds programs that pay venues for assets they already have, such as Wi-Fi, screens and space, with no new hardware or software. It reviews every program before introducing it, and most do not pass. Start with a free revenue review.

Who it is for

Built for venues with money already on prem.

Hotels, bars and lounges, restaurants, entertainment venues, bowling and family fun centers, fitness clubs, coworking spaces, multi-location retail and hospitality groups, and independent owners.

How it works

What it does, step by step.

  1. 1Assess: it looks at the venue's space, screens, Wi-Fi, guest traffic and existing setup.
  2. 2Match: it suggests only programs that bring new business or add passive revenue, and says so when a program is not a fit.
  3. 3Launch: it introduces the partner, helps with setup, and stays in touch after launch.
  4. 4Every program is reviewed before it is introduced. Mailbox Rev is selective on purpose.

What it shows

A service with a filter: it only recommends what fits, and it says so.
